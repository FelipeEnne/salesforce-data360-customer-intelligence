# Data Sources

Names, email addresses, phone numbers, and other enriched identity attributes generated by this project are synthetic. The generated files are demonstration data and must not be treated as production data.

## Olist

The public Brazilian E-Commerce dataset provides customers, orders, order items, payments, reviews, products, sellers, geolocation, and product-category translations. It is downloaded locally through Kaggle Hub and loaded into the `olist` PostgreSQL schema.

## Customer Master

`crm.customer_master` contains one record per Olist `customer_unique_id`, enriched with a synthetic name, email address, E.164 phone number, and date of birth. Generation is deterministic: the same input customer ID produces the same identity attributes.

The Customer Master is the source for generating the other channels and the internal reference used to evaluate identity relationships.

## Salesforce Contacts

`crm.salesforce_contacts` represents CRM-style contacts with a Salesforce-like Contact ID, lead source, contact status, and identity attributes. Controlled quality profiles introduce uppercase emails, formatted phones, abbreviated names, missing fields, and small typos.

The project exports a balanced sample of 1,000 contacts for the Salesforce Data Import Wizard. Its corresponding ground-truth file is for evaluation only and must not be imported into CRM.

## Support

`support.tickets` represents tickets received through email, chat, phone, or WhatsApp. Records include requester identity, category, priority, status, sentiment, creation time, resolution time, and resolution duration.

Approximately 30% of customers receive one to three tickets. Requester attributes contain the same controlled identity-quality patterns used to exercise matching.

## Marketing

`marketing.interactions` represents campaign sends, opens, clicks, conversions, and unsubscribes. About 70% of customers participate in one deterministic campaign. Email addresses can be clean, uppercase, or missing.

## Web Events

`web.events` represents `page_view`, `product_view`, `add_to_cart`, `checkout_started`, and `purchase` events. Each event belongs to an anonymous browser and session. A journey begins anonymously and can become known at checkout or purchase through an email address and internal customer reference.
